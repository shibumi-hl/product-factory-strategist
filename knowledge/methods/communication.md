# Communication strategy: method

1. **Audience and context.** Who, in what moment (searching tariffs, receiving
   a bill, in a store), with how much attention.
2. **Barriers** (from evidence, not guesses):
   - understanding — they don't get what it is;
   - relevance — they don't see it's for them;
   - trust — they don't believe it;
   - effort — switching seems costly;
   - price — value doesn't feel worth it.
3. **Key message**: one sentence, the customer's words where possible (from
   interviews/reviews), addressing the top barrier.
4. **Proof points**: each one tied to an evidence row or a product capability.
5. **Channels and roles**: awareness, explanation, conversion, onboarding,
   retention. One role per channel; say why that channel fits that role.
6. **Promise ↔ capability.** For every promise: which product capability
   delivers it, its status (delivered / planned with date / not supported),
   evidence. Unsupported promises are removed. Planned ones are not promised as present.
7. **Message testing**: how the message will be tested (A/B, preference test,
   comprehension check) — links to the validation plan.
