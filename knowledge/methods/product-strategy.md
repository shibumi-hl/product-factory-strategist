# Product strategy: method

1. **Segment by situation, not demographics.** "Families where a parent pays
   for 2–4 numbers and manages them from one app" beats "25–45, urban".
2. **Job-to-be-done.** What progress the segment wants; what they "hire" today
   (including a workaround or nothing). The current alternative sets the bar.
3. **Alternatives** the segment has: direct competitors, substitutes, doing
   nothing, doing it themselves.
4. **Direction and trade-offs.** A strategy is defined by what it refuses.
   Name at least two things you deliberately will not do and why.
5. **Value → cost → revenue.**
   - Value: which job improves, by how much, for whom (evidence).
   - Revenue mechanism: price, upsell, retention gain, cross-sell, partner fee.
   - Cost drivers: acquisition, serving, support, partner share, development.
   - Unit economics with **ranges** and stated assumptions (P). Show the break-even condition.
6. **Risks to the strategy**: demand, execution, competitive response, regulation.
