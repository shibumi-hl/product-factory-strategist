# Interviews: method

## Real interviews

- Ask about the **last specific time** something happened, not general opinions.
- Avoid: "Would you…", "Do you like…", "How much would you pay…" — people predict their behaviour badly.
- Listen for workarounds, money and time spent, emotions at moments of failure.
- 5–8 interviews per segment usually reveal the main themes; they do not measure prevalence.
- **Consent**: explain purpose, recording, anonymisation; get explicit yes.
- **Personal data**: no names, phones, emails, account numbers in the repo. Use R1, R2…

## Simulated respondents (synthetic)

What they are good for: drafting and debugging the interview guide; generating
hypotheses and objections we have not thought of; rehearsing message comprehension.

What they cannot do: prove demand, estimate shares or willingness to pay,
replace real customer evidence. Personas reflect the model's priors and our
inputs, including our biases.

Rules: persona built from cited evidence where possible; respondent agent never
sees our hypotheses; output labelled SYNTHETIC; ledger type S.
