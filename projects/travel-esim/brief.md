# Brief: Travel data eSIM for Russians going abroad

Created: 2026-11-17 · Status: confirmed by PM 2026-11-17

## Original request
> We are a Russian mobile operator. Should we launch an eSIM data product for Russians travelling abroad? Who should we sell it to, how, and what should we tell them so they understand its value? Use only public data. Agent budget for this project: $6. Keep research tight: at most ~12 sources total.

## Decision to inform
(user) Go / no-go on launching a standalone data-only travel eSIM, and if go: the first target segment, sales channel and core message for a pilot before the summer 2027 season. Decided by the product committee around Q1 2027.

## Recipient of the output
(user) The PM and the product committee; an options memo, then a strategy doc (product + comms) with a validation plan, in English.

## Target outcome and success metric
(user) Win = 300k paid activations in 12 months, with margin per activation above the roaming data revenue it replaces. Baseline: 0 activations; replaced roaming revenue unknown (internal). Cannibalisation acceptable if total margin grows (Q8).

## Status quo
Outbound travel is large and growing: 31.5 mln trips in 2025, 13.4 mln tourist (F) [E-001]; Turkey, UAE, Egypt lead (F) [E-002]. Travellers today use operator roaming, local SIMs, competitor travel eSIMs - MTS since Nov 2024 (F) [E-003], Beeline (F) [E-004] - and independent eSIM apps (H) [E-005]. (user) We are one of the big four, kept unnamed; reason from public market data (Q3). Nothing to add to alternatives (Q4).

## Target user (specific situation)
(user) Own subscribers first: eSIM-capable phone, 7-14 day leisure trip to Turkey/UAE/Egypt/SE Asia, currently avoiding roaming data (Q6).

## Constraints
- Agent budget $6; at most ~12 public sources (9 used: 4 in framing, 5 in collect).
- Public data only; no confidential operator or third-party data (AGENTS.md rule 8).
- (H) Legal/sanctions limits on foreign wholesale partners and payments; cannibalisation accepted if total margin grows (Q8, user).

## Available data and approved sources
sources/ is empty. Approved: public web (regulator/official statistics, operator press releases, reputable media, review sites at lower reliability). Freshness limit (H): 24 months.

## Premises (confirmed / rejected)
PM answers 2026-11-17: P2, P3 confirmed; P1, P4 rejected as untested -> research questions RQ-01, RQ-04.
- P1 (H) There is unmet demand that MTS, Beeline and independent eSIM apps do not already capture [E-003, E-004, E-005].
- P2 (H) The product is a separate data-only eSIM, not a cheaper roaming option on the subscriber's own number.
- P3 (H) The first buyers are our own subscribers, not all Russian travellers.
- P4 (H) Extra eSIM margin outweighs the roaming data revenue it cannibalises.

## Agent budget
$6 total; ~12 sources; evidence freshness 24 months (H).

## Assumptions (H)
- We have roaming packages but no standalone travel eSIM yet (operator unnamed; consistent with Q3).
- The output is in English.
