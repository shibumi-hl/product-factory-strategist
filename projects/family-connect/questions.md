## Frontier

### RQ-01 Do public figures support the brief's market split and need (families vs multi-number/router owners)?
- Could change: deprioritises the owners-first option if multi-SIM/router ownership needing help is a small share; supports families-first if shared plans are mass
- Method: public stats/operator reports on family tariffs, multi-SIM ownership, home-internet via SIM routers in Russia
- Sources: public web (≤24 months), Rel. A–C
- Budget: ≤6 sources · Status: in-progress

### RQ-02 What do competitors already offer for family groups and multi-number owners (shared pool, low-balance alerts, paying for relatives, router SIM management) and at what price?
- Could change: kills "paid add-on" variants if equivalents are free; shows which differentiation is left for the alert/cabinet options
- Method: public tariff/service pages of Russian operators (MTS, Beeline, T2, MegaFon) and bank auto-pay offers
- Sources: product pages (Rel. B) · Budget: ≤8 sources · Status: in-progress

### RQ-03 How large is the effect of proactive low-balance / payment alerts and of multi-line membership on churn and service interruptions?
- Could change: kills the retention-lever (P2) if effect ranges cannot plausibly reach thousands of extra paid months; sets the H range for profit model
- Method: public studies/case reports on proactive notifications, bill shock, involuntary churn; telecom churn benchmarks (monthly churn, multi-line vs single-line)
- Sources: regulator/industry/academic/consultancy, Rel. A–C · Budget: ≤8 sources · Status: in-progress

### RQ-04 What conversion rates are realistic for in-app offers, support-led offers and referrals in telecom/fintech?
- Could change: decides whether the 12 000 connected-groups target is reachable by acquisition or must come via fewer exits; picks channels
- Method: public benchmarks on push/in-app CTR and conversion, referral program conversion
- Sources: industry benchmarks, Rel. B–C · Budget: ≤6 sources · Status: in-progress
